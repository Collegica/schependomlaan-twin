# 4D model dates differ from the 18 February schedule for eight tasks

The event log gives two planned periods per task: the 4D model's and the 18 February schedule's.[^eventlog] They agree for 64 of the 72 tasks. For these eight they differ:[^eventlog]

| Task id | Task (Dutch) | Task (English) | 4D model | 18 Feb schedule | Working days in schedule |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ST00560 | Leggen breedplaat incl. Veiligheid | Lay precast floor planks incl. edge protection | 2015-05-01 to 2015-05-04 | 2015-05-01 to 2015-05-06 | 2.0 |
| ST00690 | Wapening | Reinforcement | 2015-05-13 to 2015-05-14 | 2015-05-13 to 2015-05-18 | 2.0 |
| ST01120 | Plaatsen scharnierkap incl. goot en platte daken | Install hinged roof incl. gutter and flat roofs | 2015-07-16 to 2015-08-06 | 2015-07-16 to 2015-08-27 | 16.0 |
| ST01070 | Metselwerk | Brickwork | 2015-07-22 to 2015-08-04 | 2015-07-22 to 2015-08-25 | 10.0 |
| ST00880 | Installateurs | Building services contractors | 2015-07-22 to 2015-07-27 | 2015-07-22 to 2015-08-17 | 4.0 |
| ST01130 | Plaatsen scharnierkap incl. goot en platte daken | Install hinged roof incl. gutter and flat roofs | 2015-08-28 to 2015-09-17 | 2015-08-28 to 2015-09-18 | 15.0 |
| ST01220 | Dakpannen | Roof tiles | 2015-09-04 to 2015-09-17 | 2015-09-04 to 2015-09-18 | 10.0 |
| ST01210 | Dakbedekking platte daken | Flat-roof covering | 2015-09-18 to 2015-10-01 | 2015-09-21 to 2015-10-02 | 10.0 |

Dates from the event log;[^eventlog] working days from the schedule.[^schedule]

In each case the 4D model's period ends earlier. In the schedule, several of these tasks span many more calendar days than their working days; for example, Installateurs runs 2015-07-22 to 2015-08-17 with 4.0 working days.[^schedule]

Neither source explains the difference. Whether the schedule's long spans include non-working periods, and which dates the on-time/late check used, are listed in [open questions](../open-questions.md).
