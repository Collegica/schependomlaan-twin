## Model checks

The Multicom check of 31-03-2015 records: *"Constructeur geeft op het laatste moment aan dat de balkons opgelegd moeten worden. 200mm in de kalkzandsteen van 300mm en 100."* (The structural engineer says at the last moment that the balconies must be supported: 200mm into the calcium silicate "of 300mm and 100".)[^checks] The Willemsen checks of 17-06-2015 and 19-06-2015 report roof timber intersecting the calcium silicate at the front extensions (4x), and calcium silicate not sufficiently cut out of the roof.[^checks] The Linden check of 11-05-2015 asks for interior boarding to be narrowed 22mm on both sides because it touches the calcium silicate.[^checks]
